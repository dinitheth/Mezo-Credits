Put MezoFlow on Mezo testnet

This page sends two contract creation transactions from your own wallet. It cannot move your money, and it holds no keys. Testnet only.

Checking wallet…

1. Payment contract

Moves MUSD for paid bills and writes a public receipt.

2. Lending pool

Lets people lend MUSD and borrow against Bitcoin. It reads Bitcoin's price from Mezo, and it has no owner.

What to do with the two addresses

  1. Copy them into the hosting settings as VITE_ROUTER_ADDRESS_TESTNET and VITE_VAULT_ADDRESS_TESTNET.
  2. Redeploy the app.
  3. Open the Grow page and lend a small amount of MUSD to fill the pool.
  4. Make a small bill and pay it, then borrow a small amount. Both should show a receipt.
