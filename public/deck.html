02 · Problem

The gap

People hold Bitcoin but bill in dollars.

Bills are not links

A wallet can send money, but it cannot reconcile who paid which bill.

Selling Bitcoin is a tax event

Bitcoin holders want spending money without giving up their position.

Lending is opaque

Savers cannot see cash, money out on loan, and fees in one plain view.

“Send me 25 dollars” should be as easy as sending a link.

03 · Solution

One app, three jobs

Get paid. Borrow. Lend.

1

Payment links

Make a bill in seconds. Share a link or QR. The payer pays in MUSD and the bill turns paid with a public receipt.

2

Bitcoin-backed loans

Lock Bitcoin and borrow MUSD at 150, 200 or 250% safety. The price comes from Mezo, not from us.

3

Lend to the pool

Put MUSD in. Earn 1% a year when borrowers repay. Your share counts cash and money out on loan.

07 · Users and business

Who pays for it

Start with people already paid in MUSD.

  • First users freelancers and small shops paid in MUSD; Bitcoin holders who want dollars without selling.
  • Wedge a payment link is easier to try than a new account.
  • Revenue path a small share of the 1% yearly fee, or a flat fee per paid bill. Not switched on today.

Honest risks

Pool liquidity, smart-contract risk, and reliance on Mezo’s oracle. Borrowing and closing pause while the price is stale; lending and repayment keep working. Contracts are tested but not audited.
